This notice explains our processing in plain language and, for people in Türkiye, is intended to provide the information required under Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

02Data we process

The details depend on which features you use:

  • Account and sign-in: account identifier from Apple or Google sign-in, the email address returned by the provider (which may be an Apple relay address), the first name shared by the provider where available, an app username, language, time zone and account settings. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Questions and images: the question text, the two option images you upload, audience preferences, content-sensitivity selection, creation and expiry times, and moderation status.
  • Votes and results: the selected option, a record that a vote was submitted, and (if available) an age band and gender category associated with the vote for group-level result breakdowns.
  • Optional profile details: gender and date of birth when you choose to provide them, and country or city details if you add them. Your full birth date is also used if you declare that you are over 18 to enable sensitive-content matching.
  • Device and notifications: device platform and identity, app and operating-system versions, notification preferences, and a push token if you enable notifications.
  • Safety and technical records: reports, moderation and account-deletion audit events, and request metadata such as IP address, user agent and app version needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service. Refresh tokens are stored as cryptographic hashes rather than as usable session secrets.

The app requests camera or photo-library access only when you choose to add an image to a question. It does not need access to your contacts or microphone to provide its core features.

03How we use data

  • Create and secure your account and authenticate sign-ins.
  • Show your questions to a suitable audience, receive votes and calculate your result.
  • Moderate images and text, respond to reports, prevent abuse and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • Send notifications you enable, including when a question needs an answer or your result is ready.
  • Provide support, diagnose errors, maintain the service, and meet legal obligations.

We do not use your data to serve behavioural advertising or sell it to data brokers.

04How we collect data and legal grounds

We collect information you provide in the app, information returned by your chosen sign-in provider, device details sent when the app connects, and records created as you ask, vote, report content or change settings.

Depending on the purpose and data type, we process data because it is necessary to provide the service you request, because you have chosen an optional feature or given consent where required, to pursue legitimate interests such as preventing abuse and securing the service where those interests do not override your rights, to meet legal obligations, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. The applicable condition depends on the specific processing.

05How anonymity works

Hangi? is designed to let people ask without making voting social. By default, your first name is not shown with a question. You can choose to show it in your profile settings.

The person who asked a question sees a combined result, not the identity of individual voters or a vote count. Small demographic groups are hidden where showing a breakdown could make a person easier to identify. A vote is associated with an account internally to prevent repeat voting and operate the service, but it is not shown to the asker as an identity.

When an account is deleted, vote records needed to preserve already-issued aggregate results may remain in a form that is no longer linked to that account.

06Who receives data

Other users can see the question and its option images when the question is delivered to them. They may see your first name only if you have enabled name display. They do not receive your sign-in details or an individual voter list.

We use service providers to host and maintain the app, database and image storage, process Apple or Google sign-in, and deliver push notifications. These providers receive only the information needed for their service and act under their applicable terms and our instructions where required. The production hosting and storage providers may change as the service evolves.

We may disclose information when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, or to protect users and the service. We do not sell personal data.

07International processing

Apple, Google, push-notification and infrastructure providers may process information in countries outside Türkiye, including countries where their systems or service teams operate. Where cross-border transfer rules apply, Albert Medya will use a transfer mechanism permitted by applicable law. Contact info@albertsoftware.com to ask about the service providers relevant to your account.

08How long we keep data

Questions are time-limited and stop being distributed when they close or expire. Option images are kept for a shorter period than the decision record and are scheduled for removal after they are no longer needed. Account details, device tokens and active sessions are removed or disabled when you delete your account. Security and moderation audit records may be retained for a limited period where needed to investigate abuse, meet legal duties or protect the service.

Some vote and result information may remain after account deletion in de-identified form so that completed results are not changed. We keep data only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described here or required by law.

09Your choices and rights

You can control whether your name appears with questions, whether to share optional age and gender details, and whether to receive push notifications. You can change or remove optional profile details in the app.

Subject to applicable law, you may ask whether we process your personal data, request information about that processing and recipients, ask us to correct or erase data, request notification of corrections or deletion to recipients, object to certain processing, and seek compensation where the law allows. In Türkiye, these rights are set out in Article 11 of KVKK.

Send a request to info@albertsoftware.com and tell us how to identify your account. We may ask for information needed to verify the request. You may also apply to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) as provided by law.

10Delete your account

You can start deletion in the app from Profile → Privacy & data → Delete account. You can also contact info@albertsoftware.com if you cannot access the app. Account deletion removes or disables account identifiers, profile details, sign-in links, device registrations and active sessions; active questions are removed from distribution. Information that must be retained by law, security audit records, and de-identified votes needed to preserve aggregate results may remain for the relevant retention period.

11This website and changes

This static website does not set analytics or advertising cookies and does not load third-party fonts or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process standard connection data as part of providing that hosting.

We may update this notice when the service or its data practices change. We will update the date below and, where required, notify you in the app or obtain consent.
